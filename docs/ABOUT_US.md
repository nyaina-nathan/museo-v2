# About Museo

## Our Story

Museo was founded in 2024 with a singular conviction: **football heritage belongs to everyone.**

What began as a personal passion for collecting iconic jerseys evolved into something larger—a recognition that the most legendary pieces of football history are often locked away, priced beyond reach, or disappearing from public memory altogether. We asked ourselves a question that became our mission:

*What if we could preserve these artifacts, reproduce them faithfully, and make them accessible to the collectors, the enthusiasts, and the everyday fans who genuinely cherish them?*

Museo was born from that question.

---

## What We Believe

### Football Is Identity. Memory. Cultural Pride.

Football isn't merely a sport. It's a language spoken across continents, a thread that connects generations, a mirror of national pride. When you see the yellow of Brazil, the red of Spain, the blue of Argentina—you're not seeing just colors. You're seeing history, heartbreak, triumph, and belonging.

A jersey isn't simply clothing. **It is a artifact.** It carries the sweat of legends, the roar of stadiums, the tears of fans who witnessed something unforgettable. It holds the memory of a moment when everything changed—when your nation lifted a trophy, when an underdog became immortal, when a player became a god.

**That's why jerseys matter.**

And that's why we believe they should not be locked in climate-controlled vaults, owned only by the wealthy few, or left to fade into obscurity. The stories they hold belong to everyone.

---

## Our Approach

### Why We Make Reproductions (Not Originals)

Authentic vintage jerseys are increasingly rare, fragile, and inaccessible. We made a deliberate choice: **we create museum-quality reproductions** of the most iconic designs in football history.

This choice reflects our values:

**Preservation Without Scarcity**
Authentic vintage pieces risk decay, loss, or being squirreled away in private collections never to be seen again. By reproducing legendary designs faithfully, we ensure these pieces remain part of the living conversation about football culture.

**Access Over Exclusivity**
We believe iconic football heritage should be worn, celebrated, and passed on—not locked behind price tags that only the elite can afford. A genuine collector, a passionate fan, a young person discovering their national team's legacy—all deserve to own a piece of that story.

**Craftsmanship Over Commodification**
Each jersey is commissioned with the same rigor and respect a museum curator applies to an exhibition. We partner with skilled manufacturers who understand that these aren't just products—they're cultural documents requiring precision, integrity, and care.

## How We Work

### Sourcing With Integrity

Every design we reproduce begins with **exhaustive historical research**. We consult:
- Original manufacturer archives (Nike, Adidas, Umbro, Puma, etc.)
- Match photographs and historical documentation
- Design records and technical specifications
- Collector networks and football historians

We don't guess. We don't approximate. We investigate until we can reproduce a piece with archaeological accuracy.

### Manufacturing With Care

Our manufacturing partners are based internationally—in Asia and Europe—chosen for their ability to match original materials, stitching techniques, and aesthetic details. We work closely with these partners to ensure:

- **Material fidelity** — Fabrics match the era and original specifications
- **Construction accuracy** — Seams, collars, sleeves reflect the original build
- **Visual precision** — Colors, graphics, and numbering are verified against source materials
- **Durability** — Every piece is built to last generations, not just seasons

### Presenting With Respect

Each jersey arrives as more than a product—as a **curated exhibition**.

Your piece is packaged in an archival-quality box, wrapped in museum-grade tissue, and accompanied by a **Cartel d'Exposition** (Exhibition Card) that includes:
- The year and historical context
- The legendary player who wore it
- The team and national pride it represents
- The equipment manufacturer's role in that era
- The legacy this design carries

When you receive a Museo piece, you're receiving a **curatorial statement**: this is how we believe football heritage should be honored.

## Our Mission & Values

### Mission
To preserve the cultural legacy of football by making iconic jersey designs accessible, affordable, and authentic—so that every passionate collector, devoted fan, and curious enthusiast can own a piece of the game's greatest moments.

### Values

**Raffiné (Refined)**
We treat football with the elegance of high culture. Every detail matters—from the stitching to the packaging to the language we use. There are no shortcuts; there is no hype. There is only excellence, understated and purposeful.

**Artistique (Artistic)**
Football is our muse. We celebrate the design genius of legendary equipment makers, the poetry of vintage motifs, the geometry of classic jerseys. Every piece in our collection is a work of art—and the person who wears it becomes its curator.

**Fiable (Reliable)**
In a market saturated with mediocrity, we are the guarantee of authenticity and quality. Each piece is expertly verified. Our commitment to craftsmanship is absolute. Your trust is our most precious certificate.

## Our Team

Museo operates as a small, dedicated collective united by a shared passion for football heritage and curatorial excellence. While our core team remains intentionally modest in size (allowing us to oversee every detail personally), we collaborate with:

- **Manufacturing partners** in Asia and Europe who understand the weight of these artifacts
- **Historical researchers and archivists** who verify design authenticity
- **Collectors and historians** who guide our understanding of football's visual legacy
- **Community members** in Madagascar and beyond who remind us why this work matters

We are not a faceless corporation. We are custodians of memory, working together to ensure that football's greatest moments—captured in thread and fabric—remain alive for generations to come.

## Looking Forward

### Our Vision for Global Reach

Founded in 2024 in Antananarivo, Madagascar, Museo began as a local passion. Today, we're preparing to expand beyond our borders.

**In the near term:** We will deepen our roots in Madagascar and the African continent, building a community of collectors who understand that football heritage is universal, yet deeply personal.

**In the horizon:** We aim to become a **global archive of football heritage**, accessible to enthusiasts in every corner of the world. Whether you're in Tana, Dakar, São Paulo, Madrid, or Tokyo—your connection to football's legacy should not be limited by geography or economics.

This expansion will be measured and intentional. We will not sacrifice quality for scale. We will not abandon the curatorial approach that defines us. Every new market, every new partnership, will be held to the same standard: **does this preserve the integrity of what Museo stands for?**

## Why Museo Exists

In a world where football merchandise is mass-produced, commodified, and forgettable, Museo exists to ask a different question:

**What if jerseys were treated as artifacts worthy of a museum?**

**What if iconic designs were accessible to everyone?**

**What if wearing a piece meant understanding the history, the player, the moment it represents?**

**What if football heritage could be passed from one generation to the next—carefully, respectfully, with knowledge and pride?**

That's what Museo does. Not just selling jerseys. **Archiving history.**
